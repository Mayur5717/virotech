FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CNC Milling and how does it work?

CNC (Computer Numerical Control) Milling is a subtractive manufacturing process where a rotating cutting tool removes material from a workpiece guided by pre-programmed computer code. It can produce complex 2D profiles, 3D contours, slots, holes, and surfaces with high dimensional accuracy.

What is the difference between CNC Milling and VMC Machining?

VMC (Vertical Machining Centre) is a type of CNC milling machine where the spindle axis is vertically oriented. VMCs are better suited for flat-surface, prismatic, and multi-face machining in a single setup, and typically offer higher rigidity and better accuracy compared to conventional CNC mills.

What materials can you machine?

We machine a wide range of materials including Mild Steel (MS), Stainless Steel (SS 304, SS 316), Aluminium alloys, Brass, Copper, Cast Iron, EN series steels, and various engineering plastics. Please contact us with your specific material requirement.

What is the minimum order quantity?

We accept orders starting from a single prototype piece up to high-volume production batches. There is no strict minimum order quantity — contact us with your requirement and we will be happy to assist.

Do you provide material test certificates?

Yes, we can provide material test certificates (MTC) from approved suppliers on request. We also maintain full traceability of raw materials used in your components.

How can I send my drawings for a quotation?

You can email your 2D drawings (PDF, DXF, DWG) or 3D models (STEP, IGES, SolidWorks) to contact@virotechengg.com along with quantity and delivery requirements. We will revert with a detailed quotation within 24–48 hours.
